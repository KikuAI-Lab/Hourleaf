# Product

Class: live product design contract. Owner: Hourleaf product UI. Update when
the target user, product purpose, voice, or design principles change.

## Register

product

## Users

Hourleaf is for an individual who records ministry time on an iPhone and may
have little interest in technical terminology. The primary workflow is saving
time quickly, checking monthly and service-year progress, and preparing a
monthly report without maintaining a separate ledger.

## Product Purpose

Hourleaf keeps a private, local-first record of service and credit time. It
should make daily entry nearly effortless, explain reporting rules in ordinary
language, and produce a trustworthy monthly report. Success means a person can
use Hourleaf as the only ledger for a full month and understand every total and
setting without outside help.

## Brand Personality

Calm, clear, trustworthy. The voice is concise and human, with no promotional
claims or invented warmth.

## Anti-references

- Official-looking religious organization branding or implied affiliation.
- Technical database, analytics, SDK, or synchronization terminology in
  user-facing copy.
- Decorative dashboard complexity, unexplained controls, and generic AI-style
  slogans.

## Design Principles

- Open directly into the primary task: recording time.
- Explain consequences with a short concrete example.
- Make editable controls and navigation visibly distinct from status text.
- Prefer familiar iOS controls and plain language over custom interaction.
- Preserve trust: never imply that a report was delivered or data was synced
  without evidence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, dark appearance, sufficient contrast, and
reduced motion. Copy should be understandable to a non-technical older adult
and remain natural in English, Russian, and Ukrainian.
