Hourleaf is a simple, private place to keep your ministry time.

Record service or credit time in seconds, add an optional note, and review every entry in a list or calendar. Hourleaf keeps service and credit totals separate, follows your progress through the service year, and prepares a clear monthly report you can share with the apps already on your iPhone.

On Apple Watch, choose service or credit, set hours and minutes with the Digital Crown, and save the entry to your iPhone.

With Siri and Shortcuts, you can record a spoken number of minutes or prepare monthly report text without opening Hourleaf. The report action only reads the calculated report; it never changes entries or sends anything.

Hourleaf includes:

• Fast entry for service and credit time
• List and calendar history with editing and deletion
• Monthly report preparation and sharing
• Monthly Bible-study count in the prepared report
• Service-year progress toward a 600-hour goal
• Monthly and inactivity reminders you control
• Apple Watch entry
• Siri and Shortcuts for voice entry and report text
• Local backup, restore, and CSV export/import
• English, Russian, and Ukrainian

Your records stay on your devices unless you choose to export or share them. Hourleaf has no account, ads, tracking, or third-party analytics.

Hourleaf is an independent personal tool. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any religious organization.
